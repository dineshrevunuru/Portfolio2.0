# Lorem — personal fact store intake

**Why this exists.** Roughly half of what visitors say to Lorem isn't a portfolio
question. Right now the fact store holds cost-per-customer, retention, photo
counts and cluster counts — and nothing about Dinesh as a person. So when
someone asks "what does he do when he's not doing this", Lorem correctly refuses:

> "That one's outside what I know. I've got his work — the client engagement,
> Neudesic, the coursework — not his life outside it."

Honest, but a dead end in the most common non-work turn there is. Refusing is
better than the old behaviour (deflecting back to the work) and much better than
improvising, which on this topic would mean fabricating a person.

**How to fill this in.** Answer in your own words, in full sentences, the way
you'd say it out loud to someone you just met. Don't write bullet points — Lorem
speaks these, and a bullet list read aloud sounds like a résumé. One or two
sentences each is plenty. **Skip anything you'd rather not have said to a
stranger** — everything here is public the moment it ships.

Answers land in `app/components/lorem/facts.ts` as a `PERSONAL` section under the
same guardrail as everything else, so Lorem can't drift off them.
