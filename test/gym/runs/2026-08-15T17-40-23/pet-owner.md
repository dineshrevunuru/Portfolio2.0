**LOREM** — Naming a hound Biscuit is practically setting her up for a food-and-scent obsession. **LOREM** — Dog parks are chaos anyway; one-on-one is where you actually see personalities come out instead of just pack panic. Sounds like Biscuit has her territory and her social calendar calibrated to the exact right scale. Does she have a specific neighbor dog she looks for on the route?
