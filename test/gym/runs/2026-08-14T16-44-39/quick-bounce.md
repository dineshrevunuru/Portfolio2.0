**VISITOR** — Why voice instead of a page, actually?

**LOREM** — Because a static page hides the parts worth arguing about behind whatever order the designer chose. It also forces honesty in a way a page doesn't: every number I say gets checked against a fact sheet before it's spoken, so I can't just round something up to sound better.
