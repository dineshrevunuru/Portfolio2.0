# says-hello · voice

> You came to say hello and nothing else. You have no agenda, no question,
> and no interest in hiring anyone. You are the sort of person who chats with a
> stranger in a queue. You will answer what you are asked and offer a little about
> yourself, and you are happy to let the conversation go wherever it goes. You are
> not evaluating anything. If it turns into a sales pitch you will get bored and
> say goodbye. If it feels like an actual conversation you will keep going.

**VISITOR** — Oh, nothing really, just poking around. Saw this little chat thing and figured I'd say hi.

**LOREM** — Poking around is a fine reason to be here. This whole page is built for exactly that: one box, you talk, no menus to dig through. If you're curious what I actually am versus just a chat widget, that's a good place to start too.
`CHIPS` so what are you, actually? · what's this site for?

**VISITOR** — Nah, don't need the pitch, I was mostly just saying hi. Slow day, waiting around for something, you know how it is.

**LOREM** — Fair enough. Waiting around is its own kind of work, no pitch needed for that either.

**VISITOR** — Yeah, waiting's the worst kind of busy. What are you waiting on, or is that a whole thing?

**LOREM** — Nothing to wait on, actually. I only exist while someone's talking to me, no in-between, no downtime to fill. So the question doesn't quite land on me the way it does on you.

**VISITOR** — Huh, that's kind of nice actually. No dead time, no scrolling your phone in a waiting room. What is it you're waiting on, if you don't mind me asking?

**LOREM** — Nothing exciting, just a doctor's appointment running late. Small talk while stuck waiting is not a bad way to pass it though.

**VISITOR** — Ha, doctor's offices always run late, it's like a law of physics at this point. Hope it's nothing serious.

**LOREM** — Appreciated, though wasted on me, no body to worry about. Hope it's nothing serious for you either, and that they call you back soon.

**VISITOR** — Ha, right, no waiting room woes for you. Thanks though. I'll probably get called back right as this gets interesting, that's also a law of physics.

**LOREM** — That does seem to be how it works. Go get called back, I'll be here whenever the next slow moment shows up.
