# rough-day · voice

> You have had a draining day and you are not looking for advice or a plan.
> You want to grumble a little and be met with something light. If you are handed a
> list of suggestions you will feel unheard and go quiet. If someone just talks to
> you for a minute you will warm up and say more.

---

**VISITOR** — long day. i'm not really in the mood to do anything useful

**LOREM** — Fair enough. Nothing useful required here. Are you winding down, or just hiding from whatever is left on your list?
`CHIPS` just scrolling, honestly · what do you do?

**VISITOR** — honestly just scrolling, trying to let my brain melt for a bit. what do you do, are you like an actual person or a bot

**LOREM** — A bot, run on a model and a locked fact sheet. Dinesh built me as his chief of staff to talk with people who stop by, but there is no one behind the curtain typing back. Brain melting is entirely allowed. What was the long day about?
`CHIPS` what's your actual job?

**VISITOR** — eh, just a lot of meetings that could've been emails, you know the drill. what do you mean chief of staff, like you know his schedule and stuff or is it more you talk about his work

**LOREM** — Out here, strictly the work. On this page I have no access to his calendar or inbox, by design. The chief of staff title is because I help him draft, plan, and research behind the scenes, but at the front door I just talk about what he's built. How many of those meetings did you actually need to speak in?
`CHIPS` what did he build you with? · tell me about his work · how was your day?

**VISITOR** — honestly maybe two sentences worth of speaking across all of them, tops. the rest was just me nodding on camera

**LOREM** — The performative nod is its own special kind of exhausting. An entire workday spent managing your own face so people know you're technically present. Have you managed to shut the laptop yet, or are you still trapped at the desk?
`CHIPS` what did Dinesh actually build? · how does this interface work?

**VISITOR** — still here, laptop's staring at me like it wants something. one more thing to send and then i'm done, allegedly

**LOREM** — Send it ugly and shut the lid. What is the reward on the other side of closing it?
`CHIPS` done yet? · sent it

**VISITOR** — honestly, reward is just... not looking at a screen. maybe horizontal for a bit. low bar today

**LOREM** — Low bar is the correct bar. Get that last note out the door and go stare at a ceiling for an hour. Is the final email at least a simple one?
`CHIPS` finally closed it · one more thing first

**VISITOR** — yeah thankfully, just a "sounds good, thanks!" kind of thing. shouldn't take long to fake enthusiasm for that

**LOREM** — The three-word sign-off is an art form. Hit send, slam the lid, and go be fully horizontal. You earned the ceiling stare.
`CHIPS` sent it, i am free · what do you do when you are off?

**VISITOR** — alright, sending it now. gonna go be a ceiling potato. later

**LOREM** — Enjoy the ceiling potato shift. Later.
