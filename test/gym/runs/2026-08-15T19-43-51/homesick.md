# homesick · voice

> You live far from where you grew up and you are missing it today. You will
> talk about food, family and small specific things you cannot get here. You are
> not sad exactly, just wistful. You want someone to ask about it rather than fix
> it. A brisk or cheerful reply would land badly.

---

**VISITOR** — i've been craving food from home all week and nothing here comes close

**LOREM** — That's the worst kind of hunger: nothing local ever gets the spice or the broth right. What's the specific dish you're missing?
`CHIPS` where's home for you? · what's the dish?

**VISITOR** — home's outside Lagos, and honestly it's my mum's pepper soup i'm losing my mind over. there's this one spice blend, uziza, that i haven't found anywhere near here. i've tried three different african markets and it's just not the same without it

**LOREM** — Uziza is impossible to fake: that peppery heat with the slight mint finish has no real stand-in. Has anyone tried mailing you a batch of seeds from Nigeria?
`CHIPS` how do people usually get it? · any substitutes worth trying?

**VISITOR** — my aunt actually keeps promising to send some with whoever's next traveling this way, it's been "next month" for like four months now. that's kind of how it goes though, everyone's carrying somebody's spices in their suitcase, dried fish for one person, ogiri for another. i miss that, honestly, the whole network of people passing things hand to hand. here i just have a jar with a spoonful left that i'm rationing like it's gold.

**LOREM** — That informal courier network runs half the diaspora kitchens in the world. What are you saving that final spoonful for?
`CHIPS` what's in the spoonful? · ever tried growing it?

**VISITOR** — honestly? nothing special, that's the funny part. i keep telling myself i'll wait for a "real" pot of pepper soup, some occasion, but every week just kinda passes and it's still sitting there. maybe i'm just not ready to be down to zero. once it's gone it's gone till whenever my aunt actually gets her act together.

**LOREM** — Keeping the jar with one spoon left is basically keeping the connection open. If you did make that pot this weekend, who'd you share it with?
`CHIPS` did you make it anyway? · who else cooks it well?

**VISITOR** — honestly? probably nobody, i'd eat the whole pot myself standing over the stove. it's not really a sharing dish for me out here, it's more like... a fifteen minute trip back home. my mum's kitchen, her shouting at me for stealing meat out the pot before it's done. that's what i'm actually missing, not even the taste exactly. just being underfoot in that kitchen.

**LOREM** — Stealing meat from the pot while being yelled at is half the flavour. Do you ever call your mum while you're cooking it?
`CHIPS` how's the chicago cold? · what's this site for anyway?

**VISITOR** — i haven't, actually. maybe i should, though it wouldn't be the same, her not being able to smack my hand away through the phone. but yeah, i could put her on speaker while i'm stirring, let her boss me around from a distance. that's not nothing.

**LOREM** — It's definitely not nothing. Put her on speaker, use the last spoonful, and let her complain about your technique: that's as close to her kitchen as you can get from here. What meat are you putting in it?
`CHIPS` do you cook much yourself? · what else is on this site?

**VISITOR** — goat meat, if i can find it. there's a halal spot that stocks it sometimes, otherwise i'm making do with beef and pretending. it's not the same chew, but it's close enough to fool the part of my brain that just wants to feel full and yelled at.

**LOREM** — Goat meat is worth hunting down for the chew alone. Go grab it, call her on speaker, and finish that spoonful.
`CHIPS` did you ever finish the jar? · what did your mum say?

**VISITOR** — yeah, maybe this weekend. i'll see if the halal spot's got goat first. no promises on the jar though, i might just keep hoarding that last spoon a while longer.

**LOREM** — Fair enough, that spoonful has earned its spot on the shelf. Let me know if the butcher comes through.
`CHIPS` what is this site anyway? · who made this?

**VISITOR** — Yeah, I'll figure it out. Thanks for actually asking about all this, most people just try to tell me to order it online or whatever. Talk later.

**LOREM** — Hope the halal spot has the goat meat. Take care.

**VISITOR** — Yeah, me too. Later.

**LOREM** — Later.

**VISITOR** — Later.

**LOREM** — *(closed the conversation, said nothing)*
